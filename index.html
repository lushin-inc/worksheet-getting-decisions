Doing the Work

How to Stay Out of the Child

1. Notice your emotions. Feelings come from the Child. You can't stop having them, but you can choose how you respond.

2. Separate head trash from the truth. What is my Child afraid of? What would my Adult say is true?

3. Weigh both sides. The cost of doing nothing and the risk of acting. Then help your prospect do the same.

4. Drop your needs. The Adaptive Child wants to be liked. The Rebellious Child wants to win.

KEY RULES  A sales call is not a place to get your needs met.  |  70% of selling comes from Nurturing Parent, 30% from Adult. Leave your Child in the car.

Salesperson: a person who facilitates decision making and uncovers the truth in order to help prospects act on things they need to.
